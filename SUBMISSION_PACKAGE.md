## Final paper status

The current paper is titled:

**Do Remittances Buffer Household Shocks? Evidence on Food Insecurity in Kyrgyzstan and Uzbekistan**

The repository includes the final Reader and Submission PDFs, LaTeX source files, verified references, manuscript tables/figures, research notes, checkpoints, and scripts used to build and validate the project outputs.

## Data handling

Restricted/provider-controlled household microdata and intermediate extracted data are intentionally excluded from GitHub. The repository keeps `data/raw/README.md` as the data-access placeholder and records data provenance, variable construction, and validation outputs in `research/`, `manuscript/`, `outputs/checkpoints/`, and `src/`.
